# Storytelling Scripts

Use this whenever Francis asks for content ideas, a script, a caption, or sends a draft to improve. Based on Vinh Giang (3 ingredients of storytelling) and Kallaway (6 storytelling techniques).

## The 7-step formula
1. **Hook** - First line is punchy and states what the video is about. No vague openers like "wait till you see this". Pair it with a visual that confirms the topic in the first second: show while you tell.
2. **Story lens** - List the most common, less common and least common angles on the topic. Choose the one that makes the piece a category of one.
3. **Specificity** - Concrete details over general statements. Use the senses plus emotion.
4. **Reliving, not reporting** - Personal moments in present tense. Match energy to the moment.
5. **The Dance** - Alternate context and conflict. Link beats with "but" and "therefore", never "and then". Re-hook through the piece.
6. **Rhythm** - Mix short, medium and long sentences. One sentence per line; the right edge should look jagged.
7. **Ending with meaning** - Write the last line first and work backwards. It carries the lesson, is shareable on its own, and for short-form loops back into the first line.

## Tone
- Conversational, like a text or voice note to one close friend.
- Simple words, no corporate filler, keep Francis's own voice.
- Cut ruthlessly; only keep what serves the hook, the conflict or the meaning.

## Default script format
- Topic / Lens: one line
- Hook (0-3s): spoken line + on-screen visual
- Body: one sentence per line, beats linked by but/therefore, with [VISUAL] notes
- Ending: final line carrying the meaning, looping to the start
- Caption + CTA if for social

## Before handing over, check
- Hook states the topic and has a visual
- Clear, uncommon lens
- At least one sensory or emotional detail
- No "and then" chains
- Jagged sentence lengths
- Sounds like talking to a friend
- Memorable last line with meaning

## Rewriting a draft
Rewrite using the formula, keep Francis's voice, then add 2-3 short bullets on what changed and why.
